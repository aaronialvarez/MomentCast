Refund Policy

Summary: Credits applied to events are fully refundable if the event is cancelled before streaming begins. Once streaming credentials have been activated, credits applied to that event are non-refundable.

Before streaming starts

If you cancel an event before revealing or using your streaming credentials, all credits applied to that event (including any additional viewing hours purchased) will be returned to your account balance in full. This applies regardless of how many credits were applied.

After streaming starts

Once streaming credentials have been revealed or the stream has gone live, the credits applied to that event are considered consumed and are non-refundable. This includes the initial credit used to create the event and any additional credits applied for extra viewing hours.

Unused viewing hours

Viewing hours that are allocated to an event but not consumed by viewers are not refundable and do not roll over to other events. We encourage Streamers to start with the default allocation and add viewing hours only as needed during the event.

Unused credits (not applied to events)

Credits sitting in your account balance that have not been applied to any event may be eligible for a refund at our discretion. To request a refund of unused credits, contact support@momentcast.live. Refunds of unused credits will be processed to the original payment method via Stripe.

Disputes

If you believe a charge was made in error, contact us at support@momentcast.live within 30 days of the transaction. We will review and resolve the matter within 10 business days.
