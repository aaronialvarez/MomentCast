Who We Are

MomentCast ("MomentCast," "we," "us," or "our") is a live streaming platform at momentcast.live that enables photographers, videographers, and other event professionals ("Streamers") to broadcast live events to their clients' guests ("Viewers") in real time.

This Privacy Policy describes how MomentCast collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our marketing site at momentcast.live, our streamer dashboard at app.momentcast.live, or our viewer pages at go.momentcast.live.

By using MomentCast, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

03

Google User Data

Google OAuth disclosure. This section specifically describes how MomentCast accesses, uses, stores, and shares data obtained through Google Sign-In ("Google user data"), in compliance with the Google API Services User Data Policy.

What Google data we access

MomentCast uses Google Sign-In for authentication only. When you sign in with Google, we request access to the following data:

  • Email address — used as your account identifier and for service communications.
  • Display name — used to personalize your dashboard experience.
  • Profile photo URL — optionally displayed in your account settings.

We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or any other Google service. We do not request sensitive or restricted OAuth scopes beyond basic profile and email identification.

How we use Google user data

Google user data is used exclusively to:

  • Create and authenticate your MomentCast account.
  • Display your name within your personal dashboard.
  • Send transactional emails related to your account and events (e.g., event confirmations, support responses).

We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, data brokerage, or any purpose beyond providing and improving your MomentCast experience. We do not transfer, sell, or share Google user data with any third party except as described in Section 5.

How we store Google user data

Your name, email address, and profile photo URL are stored securely in our database hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL), protected by encryption at rest and in transit. See Section 6 for details.

How to revoke Google access

You can revoke MomentCast's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions and removing MomentCast. Revoking access will not delete your MomentCast account or event data; contact privacy@momentcast.live to request full account deletion.

Limited Use policy. MomentCast's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for any purpose other than providing and improving the features you explicitly use within MomentCast.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create, maintain, and authenticate your MomentCast account.
  • Enable Streamers to create and manage live events.
  • Deliver live video streams and recorded replays to authorized Viewers.
  • Process payments and manage event credits.
  • Send transactional communications (event confirmations, receipts, support responses).
  • Monitor platform performance, diagnose technical issues, and improve the service.
  • Measure viewer engagement through aggregated, anonymized analytics.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your data for behavioral advertising, do not build advertising profiles, and do not sell or rent your personal information.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at rest: User data stored in Supabase is encrypted at rest.
  • Access controls: Database access is restricted to authenticated application processes; no public access is permitted.
  • Secure infrastructure: We operate on Cloudflare Pages and Cloudflare Workers, which enforce HTTPS for all requests.
  • Authentication tokens: OAuth tokens are short-lived and stored using secure, httpOnly mechanisms.

No security system is impenetrable. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights or freedoms, we will notify affected users promptly as required by applicable law.

08

Cookies & Analytics

Cookies we use

  • Session cookies: Used to keep you logged into your Streamer dashboard. These expire when you close your browser or sign out.
  • Authentication cookies: Set by Supabase Auth to maintain your authenticated session securely.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4 (GA4) cookies are set on viewer pages (go.momentcast.live) to measure page views and engagement. These are anonymized and do not identify you personally.

Viewers (unauthenticated)

Viewers of event pages at go.momentcast.live do not need to create an account. Their viewing experience is anonymous. GA4 may set analytics cookies; viewers can opt out using their browser's cookie settings or a browser-based ad blocker.

Third-party analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (tag G-KL4FM3L62J) on viewer pages to understand aggregate traffic patterns, and Cloudflare Analytics on all properties for performance monitoring. Neither service is given access to personally identifiable information.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Revoke Google Sign-In authorization at any time via Google Account Permissions, or contact us to withdraw other consents.
  • Opt-out of analytics: Disable GA4 tracking via your browser's cookie controls or Google's opt-out tool.

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know, the right to delete, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@momentcast.live. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

MomentCast is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact privacy@momentcast.live and we will delete it promptly.

Consistent with Google's COPPA requirements for OAuth applications, MomentCast's Streamer dashboard (which uses Google Sign-In) is intended for adult professionals only.
